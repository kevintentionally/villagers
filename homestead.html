What if we just lived like this?

We already share Sunday meals, babysit each other's kids and show up when life gets hard. This is a wild, half-serious idea to do it on the same piece of land.

The itch

Rent is brutal, and we only see each other twice a week

Most of us are paying Lower Mainland rent for places that don't feel like home. Some of us are about to have babies with no grandparents nearby. Some of us are single and quietly tired of eating alone.

A shared homestead is one answer: a few homes on one piece of land, separate front doors, one big table. Kids grow up with a crowd of aunties and uncles. Nobody has to figure out life on their own.

Who it's for

Families and singles, on purpose

Families

Your own place, your own sleep schedule. Plus built-in friends for your kids, shared childcare when you need a night off, and more hands at 6pm when everyone's melting down.

Single people

Not a guest room in someone else's life. A real place of your own, a real share and a real voice, with a community that notices if you haven't come out in a few days.

What holds it together

Core values

  • BelongingEveryone has a place at the table, married or single, kids or no kids, early riser or night owl.
  • FreedomShared life, not shared everything. Separate homes, separate budgets, and the freedom to close your door.
  • TruthWe say the hard thing early and kindly, especially about money and expectations.
  • GraceThings will go sideways. We'll get it wrong with each other, then forgive and try again.
  • DeeperFewer people, more depth. We're building a village, not a commune or a startup.

Before anyone signs anything

The questions we'd answer together

  • Who lives where, and what happens when a family outgrows their place?
  • If someone needs to leave, how do we value their share and buy them out?
  • How do we split bills, repairs and the work of keeping the land going?
  • How shared is shared? Meals and childcare, or friendly neighbours with fences?
  • Who decides things, and how do we work through disagreements?
  • Can we handle ferry life, an hour away from a hospital and big grocery stores?
