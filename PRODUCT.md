# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two client types: solopreneurs who need a capable second employee to handle operations, strategy, and execution alongside them; and leaders at mid-sized organizations (20–200 people) who need a high-trust executive partner. Visitors arrive with a concrete problem and are deciding whether to trust a person, not buy a service.

## Product Purpose

Üsan Consulting is a family practice founded by Kevin, Karina, and Deborah Chiang. The name honors Kevin's father John, who passed in 2020. Üsan (雨傘) means umbrella in Mandarin. The philosophy: walk alongside clients, holding the umbrella close, accompanying them through whatever they need to navigate. Kevin brings strategy and ENFP execution energy; Karina brings relational systems and ISTJ operational depth; Deborah brings wisdom and mentorship from a lifetime of experience.

## Brand Personality

Energetic. Real. Thoughtful. Warm without being soft. Personal without being unprofessional. Named after a family memory, run by real people with distinct personalities. Should feel like meeting a person, not reading a brochure.

## Anti-references

- Corporate law firm: stiff, formal, navy-and-gold
- Generic startup SaaS: cream backgrounds, rounded cards, Inter, hero metrics
- Luxury lifestyle brand: whisper-thin serifs, aloofness, editorial white space
- Loud agency portfolio: neon brutalism, posturing

The common thread: any aesthetic that signals "category" before "person."

## Design Principles

1. Person before product: the consultants are the service; show who they are before what they do
2. The umbrella is the idea: warmth and shelter as a feeling carried through the design, not a decoration
3. Earned trust: nothing on the page should feel like a sales move
4. Distinct voices: Kevin, Karina, and Deborah each have their own texture; the family nav connects them but their pages breathe differently
5. Origin is the brand: the 雨傘 name and John's story are the reason the firm exists; they lead

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All body text 4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion respected. Chinese characters use system CJK font fallbacks alongside the primary typeface.
