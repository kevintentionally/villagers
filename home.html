The Name

A children's song.
A father's love.

There's a Mandarin children's song — 大头大头，下雨不愁 — about a child with such a big head that they don't need an umbrella in the rain. Their head is shelter enough.

It was sung to Kevin. And the joke was always that his big head, his big ideas, his big energy — he didn't need covering.

But the truth was that his father John was always there. The umbrella. Quietly overhead, making space for everything underneath. John passed in 2020, and Üsan is named for him.
