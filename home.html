"Big head, big head — don't worry when it rains.
Others have umbrellas. I have my big head."

A children's song.
A father's love.
A firm's name.

Kevin's dad John used to sing this song to him. The joke was that Kevin's big head, his big ideas, his big energy — he didn't need an umbrella in the rain. His head was shelter enough.

But the truth was always the opposite. John was the umbrella. Quietly overhead, making space for everything underneath to breathe and grow.

John passed in 2020. Üsan — 雨傘, umbrella in Mandarin — is named for him. Every client we work with, we work with under his umbrella too.

John Chiang, 2020
"The umbrella doesn't fix the rain. It just means you don't have to face it alone."
