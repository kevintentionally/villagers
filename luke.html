A note before you read

Most pages like this one are written by the person they're about. This one isn't, and that's the point.

Glass Canvas is a relational ministry. What that means in practice is unglamorous: we pay close attention to each other, and we say out loud what we see. Gifts get named before they get titles. Vocations get spoken by the people who watched them form, not claimed by the person carrying them.

So this is a page about Luke, built out of what the people around him have named in him. We think naming vocation is the first work, not the last. If your team or your parish or your organization is trying to figure out how to honor the people inside it, this is the shape we'd offer you.
