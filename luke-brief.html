Naming Luke's Vocation

For Luke Schenk, Glass Canvas From Kevin Chiang Output a missionary page, shared as an example
●The point

Our team are first movers in naming our vocations, through being in relationship with one another. We are a relational ministry: we lift, encourage, and honor one another, and we model that for other organizations and other people.

This page is not really about Luke. It is a demonstration of what happens when a team names a person's vocation out loud. Luke is the example because the naming already happened with him.

Those may not be the right headings. The content is what matters; the structure can move.

●The turning point

He doesn't have to perform to be loved.

Luke realized he's been working with his best friends for many years, and that they don't need him to be high in competency or to perform in order for him to be loved. He's truly able to internalize that his values and who he is, that and that alone, is worthy of honor.

This is the story of how he found himself at Glass Canvas, and it's the strongest thing on the page. It's the thesis proven in one life.
